never played organised football? start here

Seven of us on the field at a time. One player, the center, starts every play by handing the ball backwards between his legs to the quarterback, who is standing about five yards behind him. That is the snap, and nothing can happen until it does.

After the snap the quarterback throws it, hands it off, or runs with it. The play ends when somebody pulls the flag off the ball carrier's belt, or the ball hits the ground, or he steps out of bounds.

We get four tries, called downs, to move the ball twenty yards. Get there and we get four more. Fail and the other team takes over. That is the entire game.

Your position is a letter. Find it in the bar above, tap it, and every instruction written for you will light up on all five pages. The glossary near the bottom of this page explains any word you hit that you do not know.
